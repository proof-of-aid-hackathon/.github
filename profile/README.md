# Proof of Aid

**Open infrastructure for humanitarian accountability.**

Proof of Aid is developing an open, provider-neutral protocol that connects verified needs, funding, settlement, delivery, and outcome evidence into one auditable history.

At its core is the **NeedClaim**: a shared record linking responsible organisations, funding conditions, milestones, and signed attestations. The proposed design keeps sensitive beneficiary information encrypted off-chain and uses blockchain to anchor records and attestations. These records make evidence traceable; verifying real-world claims still depends on trusted review.

Our proposed first milestone is a **non-custodial tracker pilot** with one NGO, one real claim, and one funder or donor group. The pilot would connect the journey from verified need to delivery and outcome evidence in a public timeline, with funds handled by existing providers.

[Read the proposal and roadmap](https://proof-of-aid.lovable.app/).

Contributions to the claim and event schemas, evidence workflows, and pilot design are welcome. Start with the proposal and our [contributing guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md), then open an issue in the relevant repository to discuss your idea.
